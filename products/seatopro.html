Powerful Features Designed For Library Owners

Seato Pro replaces manual notebooks, fee registers, and whatsapp groups with one intelligent cloud system.

Real-Time Seat Matrix

Visual floorplan grid of your reading hall. Instantly allocate seats by shift (Morning, Evening, Full Day) without double-booking.

QR & Biometric Attendance

Instant mobile QR scanning or biometric integration for student entry and exit tracking with timestamped logs.

WhatsApp Auto Alerts

Automated fee due notifications, payment receipts, late checkout alerts to parents, and library announcements sent directly to WhatsApp.

GST Receipts & UPI Collection

Generate digital GST invoices, collect monthly fees via dynamic UPI QR codes, and auto-lock seats when fees are overdue.

Shift & Locker Management

Analytics & Revenue Reports
