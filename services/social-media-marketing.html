Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Social Media Marketing process, pricing models, and timelines.

Which social media platforms are best for our business?

For B2B technology, software, and professional services, LinkedIn and Twitter (X) are the most effective. For B2C, education, e-commerce, and lifestyle brands, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook deliver the highest engagement.

Do we need to approve the posts before they go live?

Yes! We deliver complete monthly content calendars with graphics, videos, and captions in advance for your review and approval before scheduling.

Can you produce short-form video reels and TikToks?

Yes! Our video production team handles scriptwriting, dynamic motion editing, sound design, and custom captions for high-impact vertical video content.

How do you convert social media followers into paying leads?

We incorporate strategic call-to-action hooks, link-in-bio lead magnets (whitepapers, free tools, webinars), and automated DM response funnels to guide followers to your website.
