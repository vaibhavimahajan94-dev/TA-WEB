From Validated Concept to Scalable Subscription Platform

Building a successful software-as-a-service (SaaS) product requires more than clean code — it demands multi-tenant data isolation, automated billing, granular access controls, and self-serve onboarding. Techno Amaze is the technology partner behind top-tier SaaS platforms (including our flagship Seato Pro). We architect, engineer, and scale subscription software products designed to maximize Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR).

Challenges Solved

  • High upfront infrastructure costs and complex multi-tenancy database partitioning
  • Billing complexities including usage-based pricing, trials, upgrades, and churn mitigation
  • Security compliance requirements (SOC2, GDPR, ISO 27001) demanded by enterprise buyers
  • Slow feature release velocity hindering competitive market positioning

Techno Amaze Solution

  • Elastic multi-tenant architectures with automated database schema separation and pooling
  • Pre-built Stripe Billing and Paddle integration supporting tiered subscriptions and add-ons
  • Enterprise-grade Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), SSO (SAML/OAuth), and audit logs
  • CI/CD automated release pipelines ensuring daily, zero-downtime feature deployments

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our SaaS Product Development process, pricing models, and timelines.

How long does it take to launch a SaaS MVP?

Our battle-tested SaaS starter boilerplate allows us to build and launch a fully functional, production-ready SaaS MVP in just 8 to 12 weeks.

How do you ensure data security between different tenants?

We employ strict tenant isolation mechanisms, including schema-based or row-level tenant key constraints (RLS) in PostgreSQL, along with encrypted JWT tokens and tenant-scoped database connections.

Can you handle enterprise features like SSO and custom domains?

Yes! We build enterprise-ready SaaS platforms with SAML/OAuth Single Sign-On (Okta, Azure AD), custom CNAME domain routing with automatic SSL provisioning, and audit logs.

Who owns the SaaS code and database?

You retain 100% full intellectual property and source code ownership. We hand over all repositories, deployment scripts, and credentials upon completion.
