Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Content Marketing process, pricing models, and timelines.

Is your content written by real humans or generated by AI?

All our content is 100% written and edited by experienced human content strategists and domain experts, adhering strictly to Google E-E-A-T and helpful content guidelines for maximum ranking power.

How does content marketing generate actual sales leads?

We engineer content with strategic conversion points: embedded lead magnets (calculators, checklists, whitepapers), case study proof, and contextual calls-to-action that guide readers to book a consultation.

Do you handle uploading and formatting articles on our CMS?

Yes! We format articles directly in your CMS (WordPress, Webflow, Ghost, custom), configure metadata, insert custom graphics, and optimize internal links for complete turnkey delivery.

How do you ensure the content matches our unique brand voice?

During onboarding, we build a customized Brand Voice & Style Guide documenting your preferred terminology, tone, target audience personas, and formatting rules.
