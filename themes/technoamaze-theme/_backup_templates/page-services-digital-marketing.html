Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our Digital Marketing process, pricing models, and timelines.

How quickly can we expect to see results from digital marketing?

Paid advertising campaigns (Google Ads, Meta Ads) start generating qualified leads within the first week of launch. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) builds compounding organic traffic over a 3 to 6-month timeframe.

How do you measure and report marketing success?

We focus on bottom-line business metrics: Qualified Leads, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and Revenue. You will receive a real-time Looker Studio dashboard and weekly video walk-throughs.

Do you create the ad creatives and landing page copy?

Yes! Our in-house creative team writes all advertising copy, designs high-converting graphics/video assets, and builds dedicated landing pages.

Is there a long-term contract requirement?

We offer flexible month-to-month and quarterly growth retainers. We believe in retaining clients through proven performance and measurable ROI, not rigid lock-in contracts.
